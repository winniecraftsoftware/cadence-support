# cadence Privacy Policy

Effective: October 26, 2026

cadence ("the app") handles your records of chores and joys only on your device and in your own iCloud. The developer does not collect your data.

## 1. Information the developer collects

**None.** The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools.
Your items, records, notes, photos, and settings are never sent to the developer's servers or anywhere else.

## 2. Information handled on your device

The app uses and stores the following on your device to provide its features.

| Information | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Items, cycles, and notes you enter | Shown in Up next, the timeline, and records |
| Records (dates, skips, postponements) | Used to adjust cycles and calculate the next target date |
| Photos (only ones you choose or take) | Attached to and shown with your records |
| Settings such as notification time | Used to tailor how the app works for you |

If you use device backups (such as iCloud Backup), this information is included in your own backup like any other app data.

## 3. When the app communicates externally

- **iCloud sync**: The information above is stored in your own iCloud (Apple's CloudKit private database) and synced between devices signed in with the same Apple ID. The developer cannot see or access this data. It is handled under Apple's privacy policy.
- **Notifications**: Reminders are created and scheduled on your device and are not sent anywhere.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

Because the developer holds none of your information, nothing is shared with third parties.

## 5. Exporting and deleting your data

- Export: Settings → Export lets you save all items and records as a file (optionally including photos).
- Delete: "Delete this item" on an item's screen removes it with its records and photos, including from your other synced devices. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. Data remaining in iCloud can be deleted from Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage on your iPhone.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children. The developer collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with the revision date.
