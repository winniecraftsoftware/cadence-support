# cadence Support

**Life on your own beat**

cadence helps you keep the recurring chores and joys of daily life — haircuts, contact lens changes, cleaning, restocking supplies — on cycles you decide yourself. As you record, each cycle gently adjusts to your actual intervals. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

## FAQ

**Can I add something without knowing its cycle?**
Yes — just enter a name. After two records, the interval between them becomes its cycle.

**My cycle changed on its own.**
cadence adjusts cycles to match the intervals you record. Tap "Undo" in the message right after recording to revert. To stop adjustments for an item, turn on "Fix the cycle" on its screen.

**What's the difference between Skip and Postpone?**
Use Skip when you didn't need it this time, and Postpone when you couldn't do it and want to move the date. Neither is used to calculate the cycle.

**Does it sync with my other devices?**
Yes, between devices signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID. Check the status in Settings → Sync.

**Switching to a new iPhone?**
Sign in with the same Apple ID and your data comes over from iCloud. You can also keep a file copy with Settings → Backup → Export, and bring it back with Import.

**How much does it cost?**
The download is free, and every feature is available for the first 30 days. To keep recording and adding after that, a one-time purchase is required (not a subscription). Without purchasing, you can always view and export the records you already have.

**How do I purchase?**
Go to Settings → Purchase → Buy. After the 30 days, tapping record or add also opens the same purchase screen.

**I purchased, but another device or a reinstall shows it as not purchased.**
Tap Settings → Purchase → Restore Purchase. If you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to buy, it's restored at no extra cost. Family Sharing is supported.

**I'm not getting notifications.**
Check that notifications are allowed in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → cadence. Notifications arrive as one daily summary of what's coming up, at the time you choose in cadence's Settings → Notification time. The app icon shows how many chores have reached their target date (joys aren't counted). Items without a cycle yet (recorded only once with no cycle set) don't get notifications.

**What's the note at the top of Home?**
That's Today's Suggestion — a short note on what's worth doing today, based on the items coming up. On devices with Apple Intelligence it's written by the on-device model; on other devices a fixed sentence is shown. Either way, nothing leaves your device.

**Can I use it with Siri or Shortcuts?**
In the Shortcuts app, cadence offers "Coming Up", "Record", "Undo Today's Record" and "Open Item". To use them with Siri, the most reliable way is to make a shortcut with them and say that shortcut's name. Items also appear in Spotlight. After the 30 days without purchasing, "Record" and "Undo Today's Record" aren't available ("Coming Up" and "Open Item" still are).

**How do I delete data?**
Use "Delete this item" on an item's screen. Deleted data can't be restored.
