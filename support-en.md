# cadence Support

**Life on your own beat**

cadence helps you keep the recurring chores and joys of daily life — haircuts, contact lens changes, cleaning, restocking supplies — on cycles you decide yourself. As you record, each cycle gently adjusts to your actual intervals. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

## FAQ

**Can I add something without knowing its cycle?**
Yes — just enter a name. After two records, the interval between them becomes its cycle.

**My cycle changed on its own.**
cadence adjusts cycles to match the intervals you record. Tap "Undo" in the message right after recording to revert. To stop adjustments for an item, turn on "Fix the cycle" on its screen.

**What's the difference between Skip and Postpone?**
Use Skip when you didn't need it this time, and Postpone when you couldn't do it and want to move the date. Neither is used to calculate the cycle.

**Does it sync with my other devices?**
Yes, between devices signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID. Check the status in Settings → Sync.

**Switching to a new iPhone?**
Sign in with the same Apple ID and your data comes over from iCloud. You can also keep a file copy with Settings → Export.

**How do I delete data?**
Use "Delete this item" on an item's screen. Deleted data can't be restored.
